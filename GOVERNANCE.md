## Contributions

- Issues and PRs welcome on open-source crates
- All contributions must pass: `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy`, `cargo test`
- PRs require review by maintainer
- By contributing, you agree to license your work under MIT/Apache 2.0

## Release process

- Versioned according to semver
- Changelog maintained in `CHANGELOG.md`
- Release candidates tagged as `vX.Y.Z-rc.N`
- Stable releases tagged as `vX.Y.Z`
